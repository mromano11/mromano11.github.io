Chapter 6 of 8

What changed after the workshops?

The analysis examines changes in language, conceptual framing, and themes across pre- and post-training responses.

What this chapter covers

Methods include textual similarity, Bloom’s taxonomy classification, sentiment, descriptive statistics, and thematic analysis. The final conservatively reconciled paired participant cohort is small, so findings require careful interpretation.

The takeaway: Exploratory shifts in how participants express ideas are not the same as proof that an intervention works at scale.

Why it matters outside academia

Emergency systems are developed and used by people working under real organizational constraints. Understanding the communication behind the technology can help teams ask better questions before software decisions become expensive or consequential.
