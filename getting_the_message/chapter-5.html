Chapter 5 of 8

How do you test better communication?

The research uses structured interdisciplinary cross-training with discussions, collaborative exercises, reflection, and emergency scenarios.

What this chapter covers

A convergent mixed-methods design brings together surveys, workshop language, computational text analysis, and qualitative interpretation.

The takeaway: No single metric can capture the full meaning of a conversation.

Why it matters outside academia

Emergency systems are developed and used by people working under real organizational constraints. Understanding the communication behind the technology can help teams ask better questions before software decisions become expensive or consequential.

Plain-language interpretation of Chapter 5, not a substitute for the full dissertation. Source: Romano, Getting the Message, 2026 draft.
