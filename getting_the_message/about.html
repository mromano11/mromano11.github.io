Research by Maddalena Romano

Getting the Message explores communication across emergency management and technology professions, with a focus on human-centered, usable, and resilient systems.

About this website

This public companion translates dissertation chapters and associated studies into accessible language. Examples labeled illustrative are teaching devices, not research observations. The site is not an emergency information service.

Scholarly source
