Why does emergency software miss the mark?

Emergency technology is not just about servers and software. It depends on people translating urgent operational needs into systems that remain usable when conditions change.

What this chapter covers

The dissertation introduces the communication problem, research questions, human-centered design, resilience, and equity.

The takeaway: A system can be technically operational yet poorly suited to the decisions people need to make.

Why it matters outside academia

Emergency systems are developed and used by people working under real organizational constraints. Understanding the communication behind the technology can help teams ask better questions before software decisions become expensive or consequential.

Plain-language interpretation of Chapter 1, not a substitute for the full dissertation. Source: Romano, Getting the Message, 2026 draft.
