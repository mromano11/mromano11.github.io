Chapter 3 of 8

Same mission. Different mental models.

Knowledge silos form when important operational and technical knowledge remains within professional or organizational boundaries. Shared vocabulary does not guarantee shared meaning.

What this chapter covers

The chapter examines knowledge silos across emergency management and IT, then develops recommendations for design, infrastructure, training, and cross-disciplinary collaboration.

The takeaway: Cross-training is not about turning emergency managers into programmers. It is about building enough shared understanding to collaborate.

Why it matters outside academia

Emergency systems are developed and used by people working under real organizational constraints. Understanding the communication behind the technology can help teams ask better questions before software decisions become expensive or consequential.
