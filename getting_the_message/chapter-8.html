Chapter 8 of 8

Getting the message—and what comes next

The dissertation argues that better emergency technology begins with understanding the people, language, and organizations behind the requirements.

What this chapter covers

The conclusion draws together the research contributions, limitations, implications, and directions for future work.

The takeaway: Designing resilient systems requires attention to relationships as well as infrastructure.

Why it matters outside academia

Emergency systems are developed and used by people working under real organizational constraints. Understanding the communication behind the technology can help teams ask better questions before software decisions become expensive or consequential.
