Getting the Message

When an emergency strikes, technology matters. So does the conversation between the people who build it and the people who depend on it.

Explore the dissertation →
The big idea

Two groups. One mission. Different languages.

Emergency management professionals understand what must happen during a crisis. Technology specialists understand how systems are built. But sharing a goal doesn't mean sharing the same assumptions, vocabulary, or priorities.

The central question: Can structured cross-training help emergency management and IT professionals understand one another well enough to design better technology?
