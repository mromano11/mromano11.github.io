No jargon required

Useful words, made simple

Emergency management (EM)

Planning for, responding to, and recovering from emergencies and disasters.

Information Technology and Telecommunications (ITT)

The professionals and systems responsible for software, networks, data, and communications technology.

Business requirements

Descriptions of what people need a system to accomplish and why.

Knowledge silo

Knowledge that stays inside a team or profession instead of being shared across boundaries.

Human–Computer Interaction (HCI)

The study and design of how people use and experience technology.

Cross-training

Learning about another profession’s work, language, and constraints to collaborate more effectively.

Mixed methods

Using quantitative and qualitative evidence together to understand a research question.

Natural language processing (NLP)

Computational techniques for analyzing human language.

Bloom’s taxonomy

A framework for describing different levels or types of cognitive activity.

Resilience

The ability to adapt, continue functioning, or recover when conditions are disrupted.
