Chapter 2 of 8

Five problems that can feed one another

The cycle of disparity connects role and infrastructure drift, incomplete requirements, poorly understood technological weaknesses, unsuitable user interfaces, and gaps in digital resilience.

What this chapter covers

This paper-based chapter uses a gap analysis to explain how seemingly separate weaknesses reinforce one another.

The takeaway: Fixing only the interface may not fix the organizational misunderstanding that produced it.

Why it matters outside academia

Emergency systems are developed and used by people working under real organizational constraints. Understanding the communication behind the technology can help teams ask better questions before software decisions become expensive or consequential.
